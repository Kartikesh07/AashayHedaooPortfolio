# PLAY/FORM design plan

Palette: Electric Papaya #FF704A; Ultraviolet #5231B5; Misty Lavender #F1EDFF; Butter Yellow #FFE27A; Fresh Mint #A4E9D3; Deep Plum #261841.
Type: Bricolage Grotesque for expressive, tightly tracked display; DM Sans for readable supporting text and controls.

Composition: full-bleed typographic poster, PLAY / FORM spanning the viewport with isolated original game imagery inset into the letter composition. Identity and introduction sit below the poster, not beside a stack of screenshots. Quiet compact navigation.

Work: an exhibition stage with one large original artwork or controllable gameplay preview; typographic project rail along its right edge. Mobile switches to vertical project panels. Original case studies expand in place, preserving every source text/link, local document, and media item.

Process: violet desk with three movable authentic level-layout artifacts; reset, focus/keyboard reposition controls, image expansion, and complete static archive. Touch receives a static layout.

About: mint magazine spread, oversize editorial opening, flowing biography, asymmetrical experience/achievement/tool panels. Contact: violet typography and butter circular email action.

Comparison: existing interface has a navy two-column hero, two stacked phone images, small project index with three floating screenshots, and uniform centered sections. New composition removes that hierarchy: poster dominates, stage precedes navigation, process is a contrasting spatial canvas, About becomes a different-width editorial spread. No reused hero frames, palette, or project-card grid.

Motion: one masked poster entrance, independent shallow pointer parallax, user-driven project wipe, tactile bounded dragging, magnetic email action. Reduced motion disables decorative movement.
