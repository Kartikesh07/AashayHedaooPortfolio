# **1\. Level Identity & Core Idea**

| Attribute | Description | Why It Matters |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| **Level Name** | **The Wobble Arena** | Implies chaos, physics madness |
| **Map Type** | **Perfectly symmetrical square arena** | Both teams (Pink & Blue) start with equal access to walls, bushes, and teleporters. |
| **Mode** | **Wobble Bomb** | Fast 5v5 elimination with constant bomb spawns. |
| **Visual Style** | **Low-poly wobble physics** | Simple shapes help players focus on movement, explosions, and sneaky plays. |

**Identity Summary:**  
 A chaos-filled, stealth-heavy, physics-driven arena designed for hiding, ambushing, surprising, and outplaying opponents.

---

# **2\. Geometry & Obstacles (How the Map Feels to Play)**

The Wobble Chamber mixes **solid blocky walls** with **dense hiding zones** to create unpredictable fights.

### **Gray/Black Walls — Full Barriers**

* Stop movement, bomb throws

* Create safe corners and narrow chokepoints.

* The only true places to avoid explosions.

**Use:** Defensive retreats, hiding from incoming bombs, funneling enemies.

###  **Green Bush Areas — Stealth Zones**

* Players inside bushes are **completely invisible** to anyone outside.

* But bombs **can** go in and out freely.

* Perfect for:

  * Fuse-running safely

  * Ambushes

  * Sudden surprise throws

  * Hiding low-health teammates

**Use:** Sneak, wait, strike.

###  **Red Circles — Fixed Early Bomb Spawn Points**

* These are the **spots** where bombs appear instantly after setup.

* Makes the opening 10–15 seconds predictable but highly competitive.

**Use:** Early pressure and fights break out instantly.

# **3\. Interactive Elements (Spawns & Teleporters)**

### **White Cylinders — Player Starting Pads**

* Available after every 10 sec of use.

* Positioned close so teams naturally start together.

 **Pink Teleporter Pair**

* Connects two far-away pad

* Used for:

  * Deep flanks

  * Escaping danger

  * Jumping to the enemy side quickly

  * Cutting off opponents from behind

**Effect:** Turns the fight upside down instantly.

###  **Green Teleporter Pair**

* Connects symmetrical flank points.

* Allows players to jump **directly into bushes** on the enemy’s side.

**Effect:** High-level ambushes, instant pressure, and stealth repositioning.

###  **Targeting Circle (Dynamic Bomb Spawner)**

* Appears only after the initial chaos.

* Spawns bombs **randomly** on clear ground (never inside bushes or walls).

* Forces both teams to constantly move and fight for bomb control.

# **4\. Gameplay Zones & Player Flow**

### **1️ Open Central Corridors**

* Fastest routes through the map.

* Fully exposed — no hiding.

* Players moving here are at risk of:

  * Long-range throws

  * Sudden pushes

  * Bush ambushes

**Role:** High-speed chases, quick rotations, risky combat.

### **2️ The Bush Complex**

* Massive interconnected stealth zones.

* Allows teams to disappear and reappear unpredictably.

* Perfect for:

  * Fuse-running bombs

  * Hiding weakened players

  * Setting up instant “pop-out throws”

**Role:** Stealth, mind games, surprise attacks.

### **3️ Outer Wall Lanes**

* Tight chokepoints near the teleporters.

* Safer areas for:

  * Resetting a chase

  * Waiting out Push (Flail) cooldowns

  * Throwing bombs safely across teammates

**Role:** Defense, regrouping, bait-and-switch plays.

# **5\. Dynamic Elements & How the Level Paces the Match**

###  **Dynamic Bomb System**

The map always keeps the pressure high by limiting how many bombs can exist at once.

More players alive \= more bombs  
 Fewer players alive \= fewer bombs (more tension)

It ensures:

* No slow rounds

* No empty runs

* Always something to chase, avoid, or throw

###  **Poison Cloud (Endgame Forcer)**

Triggers when:

* 60 seconds pass OR

* Only 6 players remain total

It slowly removes the **outer bush areas**, forcing players inward.

**Effect:**

* No more stealth

* No more hiding

* A guaranteed final showdown in the open central corridors
